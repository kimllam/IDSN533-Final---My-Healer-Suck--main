MY HEALER SUCKS! — EXPANDED ORIGINAL SOUND PACK

15 original additional WAV files plus the 9 previously generated WAV files.
All audio in this archive is procedurally synthesized for this project, not sampled from a third-party copyrighted recording.
Files may be used, modified, and included with your game without attribution.

SOUND DESIGN: whimsical medieval fantasy / tactile wood & cards / soft bells & magic / humorous events.

NOTES
 - Existing music unchanged.
 - Adding these files alone does not make them play. Hook each filename to the matching event in AudioManager/GameManager/GameBoardView.
 - Default ambience should be quiet relative to music; test on actual iPhone.
 - WAV, mono, 24 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
 - Some tracks are simple synthesized sketches, not professionally recorded Foley. Audition before submitting.
